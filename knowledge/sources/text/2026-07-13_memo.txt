MACRO THEMES
June NFP: +57K Miss (exp. 110K+)
Worst miss since late 2025. Prior months revised -74K combined.
Unemployment to 4.2% but driven by participation collapse
(61.5%, lowest since Mar 2021). Household employment fell
507K. Wage growth 3.5% YoY. Dollar fell, gold +2%, rate hike
bets pared.
Iran: Ceasefire Cancelled — Oil Surging
Iran struck Al-Rekayyat (Qatari LNG tanker) July 7. US hit 80+
Iranian targets July 8. Trump declared ceasefire 'over.' Weekend:
US 4th strike in a week after Cyprus-flagged tanker attack. WTI
+4.6% today, Brent ~$79.50. Hormuz shipping slowed
significantly.
FOMC Minutes: 'No Urgency to Hike'
June minutes (released July 8): committee acknowledged hot
inflation but saw no urgency to hike as labor cools. NY Fed's
Williams: policy 'in a good place,' not eyeing July hike. September
hike probability now ~60-70% after oscillating post-NFP.
Equities: Dow ATH, AI Fatigue Persists
S&P; +1%, Nasdaq +2% last week — second consecutive
positive week. Dow hit intraday ATH July 6. But S&P; tech sector
-10% from recent peak. Q2 earnings growth forecast: 23.6% YoY.
Big bank earnings kick off Tuesday alongside CPI.
CRYPTO THEMES
BTC ETF 8-Week Outflow Streak Ends
Week of July 7-11: $197M BTC ETF inflows + $84M ETH =
$282M combined. Ended 8 consecutive weeks and $9.46B in
total outflows. Single-day peak: $265M on July 7. But $282M
barely dents $9.46B hole. Avg ETF holder cost basis: ~$83,800,
well above current price.
Bitcoin: Recovery Off $59K Lows
BTC at $63,042, up ~5.5% from June 29's $59,800 and ~7% from
the June 25 flush low of $59,023. USDT dominance pulled back
from 4-yr highs (9.35% → 8.54%), positive signal for risk appetite.
Resistance: $65K. Support: $61,000-$61,750.
ETH Outperforms Off the Bottom
ETH at $1,820, up ~16% from the $1,565 low two weeks ago.
ETH ETF inflows proportionally stronger vs BTC. ETH/BTC ratio
recovering from multi-year lows at 0.026. Glamsterdam devnet on
track. BitMine still holds 5.62M ETH (4.66% of supply).
CLARITY Act: August 7 Senate Deadline
Congress goes on recess August 7. If no vote, bill pushed to
midterm cycle. CFTC Chair Selig pressuring Senate. Partisan
friction around Trump's $1.4B digital asset revenues. This is 3.5
weeks away — watch the Senate floor schedule closely.
