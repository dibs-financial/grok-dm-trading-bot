BITCOIN $65,600 LEVEL + JACKSON HOLE COUNTDOWN
$65,600 — The Line That Matters: Every rally this year
has failed at or near the 50-month EMA at $65,600.
Bitcoin tagged $65,300 on Friday and pulled back.
155,000 BTC accumulated $62-65K. Soft CPI = those
holders profitable = reflexive bid above the EMA. Hot
CPI = underwater = selling pressure. A sustained close
above $65,600 would be the most significant technical
development Bitcoin has seen this entire cycle.
Jackson Hole Aug 27-29 (17 days): With NFP soft and
September hike at 44%, Warsh has room to be
balanced at the podium rather than explicitly hawkish.
The theme 'Financial Innovation: Implications for
Payments and Policy' is a direct invitation for him to
address digital assets, stablecoins, and CBDC
frameworks. Any Warsh language on Bitcoin's role in the
payment ecosystem would be historically significant —
the first time a Fed Chair has addressed it from the
Jackson Hole stage.
