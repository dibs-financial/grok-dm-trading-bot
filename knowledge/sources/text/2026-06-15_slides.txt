HOW TO READ THESE NUMBERS
•
Energy drove 60%+ of the monthly CPI increase (+3.9% MoM / +23.5% YoY)  -  entirely Iran/Hormuz supply shock, not demand-side.
•
Core CPI at 2.9% confirms this is NOT a broad-based wage-price spiral. That matters enormously for the Fed's calculus.

months.
