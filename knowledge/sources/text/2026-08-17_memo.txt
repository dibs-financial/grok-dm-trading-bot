the signal that AI trade is priced for perfection. Warsh's

history. Theme: 'Financial Innovation + Payments +

digital payments from the Jackson Hole stage.

sales all soft. The data gives him cover to be balanced

be the best outcome for risk assets into September.

stablecoins, or Bitcoin's role in the payment ecosystem
would be historically unprecedented. Even neutral
framing signals the Fed is engaging with the topic.
