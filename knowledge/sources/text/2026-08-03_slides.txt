THE VULNERABILITY EXPLAINED
Affected
Coldcard Mk3, firmware 4.0.1-4.1.9 (March 2021)
The flaw
Software RNG used instead of hardware RNG for key 
generation
How exploited
Private keys mathematically reconstructed using device serial 
numbers — no physical device needed
Who is at risk
Anyone who generated their seed phrase on an affected 
Coldcard before the 2021 firmware fix
Fix
Patched firmware released Aug 1. Update immediately. Move 
funds from potentially compromised wallets
Not at risk
Bitcoin itself is secure. This is a hardware wallet vendor 
implementation flaw, not a protocol issue
ON-CHAIN READ: HOLDERS MIGRATING, NOT PANIC SELLING
CryptoQuant: BTC sending addresses spiked to early-2024 levels on Friday. Exchange reserves rose from 2.706M to 2.718M BTC — a 12K BTC increase (modest). Glassnode: 
holders are moving coins to NEW wallets after learning their Coldcard may be compromised — not selling. BTC has held relatively flat despite the hack magnitude. The market is 
treating this as a wallet migration event, not a sell event.
