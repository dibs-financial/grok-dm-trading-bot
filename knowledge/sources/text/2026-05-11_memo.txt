CRYPTO OVERVIEW
BITCOIN
BTC closed the week above $80,600 for the first time since its Q1 correction, clearing a key technical pivot that has capped the rally
since mid-March. Spot ETFs extended their inflow streak to five consecutive weeks, bringing the two-month total to approximately
$2.78B. However, cumulative ETF net inflows ($58.7B) remain ~$2.5B below the October 2025 cycle peak of $61.2B — recovery is
real but incomplete. On-chain, long-term holders from the 2-3 year cohort have been distributing into the ETF bid in near real time.
Whether institutions are accumulating or absorbing exit liquidity is the central unresolved question.
Key levels: $80,600 pivot (hold = bullish); $85K next target; $75K invalidation.
ETHEREUM
ETH traded in the low $2,300s, roughly flat week-over-week. The institutional bifurcation story is the headline: BTC ETF inflows
continued while ETH ETFs snapped their positive streak with net outflows for the week of May 1. DeFi TVL across ETH and its L2
stack remains healthy at $68B, and approximately 33% of ETH supply is staked, constraining float. Price is still ~50% off the
October 2025 peak. ETH historically follows BTC in institutional adoption cycles but with a lag.
Watch: $2,300 support. Break below opens $2,200. ETF inflow resumption is the catalyst needed for upside.
BROADER MARKET STRUCTURE
BTC dominance holds near 58.5%, a headwind for broad altcoin rallies. Altcoin ETF flows (XRP, SOL) were negligible last week
after a strong mid-April surge. Fear & Greed has recovered from 26 to ~46 (Fear). The total crypto market cap has rebounded to
~$2.81T from Q1's $900B drawdown but remains below cycle highs. Liquidity is becoming more selective rather than purely
speculative.
MACRO OVERVIEW
FED LEADERSHIP TRANSITION
Powell's term as Fed chair ends May 15. Kevin Warsh cleared the Senate Banking Committee on a party-line vote; full Senate
confirmation vote could come as early as today (May 11). Warsh inherits a difficult mandate: Trump expects rate cuts, but core
PCE is at 3.2% and headline CPI jumped to 3.3% in March, driven by the Iran war energy shock. Cleveland Fed hawks (Hammack,
Kashkari, Logan) have been signaling no rush to ease. Powell is staying on the Board of Governors through at least part of 2028 —
unusual, and adding political tension. Watch Warsh's first public remarks as chair for any signals on rate path.
HORMUZ / OIL / INFLATION

supply disruption in IEA history (~20% of global seaborne oil trade). Brent peaked near $114 mid-week before settling ~$96 on
partial de-escalation signals. Trump's 'Project Freedom' navy escort operation launched May 4, was paused May 6 on 'great
progress,' then Iran rejected the US proposal over the weekend. Gold pulling back toward $4,700 on ceasefire optimism but

BOTTOM LINE
The path of least resistance for risk assets has been up since mid-April. Structural tailwinds (ETF institutional flows, dollar
weakness, AI earnings, labor resilience) are real. Structural risks (hot inflation, Hormuz uncertainty, rate-cut expectations deferring,
Fed political tension) are also real and not fully priced. That combination typically produces choppy but directionally positive action,
until a catalyst breaks the balance. The three things that matter most this week: (1) April CPI tomorrow morning, (2) Warsh's first
remarks as Fed chair Thursday, and (3) whether BTC holds $80,600 on a daily close basis. Those three data points will tell you
whether the current setup extends or resets.
