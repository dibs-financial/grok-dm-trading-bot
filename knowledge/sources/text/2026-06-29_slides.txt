WHY GOLD IS FALLING
Rate Hike Pricing
3 hikes now priced by year-end. Gold yields nothing — bonds become relatively more attractive 
when rates rise.
DXY at 14-Month Highs
Dollar at ~101.3 makes gold expensive for non-US buyers, suppressing global demand.
Oil Retreat Reduces 
Geopolitical Bid
Gold's war premium deflates as Hormuz traffic recovers and ceasefire holds (tentatively).
Falling Real Yields Needed
For gold to rally, either: (1) inflation expectations fall faster than nominal yields, or (2) a growth 
scare resets hike narrative.
What Could Reverse It
Weak NFP Thursday → rate hike odds fall → dollar softens → gold bounces. This is the fastest 
path.
STRUCTURAL THESIS UNCHANGED:
US debt >$37T, CB buying near record pace, dollar reserve share declining. This is a cyclical pullback inside a structural bull market — 
not a reversal.
