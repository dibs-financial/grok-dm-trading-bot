READING THE REPORT: WHAT THE NUMBERS ACTUALLY SAY
The -50K in local government education is seasonal
Summer school timing shifts cause this every July. Expected to largely reverse in August's numbers. Private sector actually increased modestly.
The revisions are the real story
May was +129K, now +63K. June was +57K, now +20K. Combined -103K. The three-month average is now ~+20K/month — not a healthy labor market.
Wages at 3.2% are the disinflationary gift
Coming in below both the 3.5% consensus AND the prior 3.5% reading. Wage growth cooling alongside a negative payroll print gives Warsh the justification to hold.
The 4.1% unemployment rate is misleading
It fell from 4.2% but only because participation dropped to 61.4%. Fewer people looking for work — not more people finding it. Not a bullish signal.
