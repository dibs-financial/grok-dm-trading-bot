MACRO THEMES
1. Fed Turns Hawkish Under Warsh
Rates held at 3.5%-3.75% at June 16-17 FOMC (Warsh's debut).
9 of 18 officials now project a 2026 rate hike. Markets pricing
~50% probability of 25bp hike in September. Rate cut thesis is
dead for now.
2. Dollar at 1-Year Highs
DXY hit ~101 — strongest since May 2025. Driven by rate-hike
repricing and safe-haven demand as Iran talks stalled. Dollar
strength is a direct headwind for commodities and risk assets.
3. Gold Correcting, Bull Case Intact
3rd consecutive weekly decline. ~25% below Jan 2026 ATH of
$5,589. Goldman cut year-end target to $4,900. Structural thesis
(CB buying, $37T US debt, dollar debasement) unchanged —
short-term pressure from higher real yields is the driver.
4. Oil: Iran Talks Are the Swing Factor
WTI ~$77. Down from $120+ war peak. Friday's Geneva talks
collapsed. Oil ticking higher this morning. Strait of Hormuz still
below pre-war traffic. Energy-driven inflation (CPI 4.2% YoY May)
keeps rate cuts suppressed.
CRYPTO THEMES
1. BTC: ETF Outflows + Leverage Flush
May ETF outflows: $2.3B (largest of 2026). June 5 saw $1.5B
long liquidations as BTC broke $62K. Now grinding back near
200-day MA at ~$63,800. RSI neutral (49.74). MACD negative.
Key line: $62K support.
2. On-Chain: Cautious But Not Broken
Long-term holders began distributing in late May. Funding rates
normalized post-flush (crowded longs removed). MVRV in neutral
zone for BTC. Not screaming buy — not freefall either.
3. ETH: Fundamentals vs. Price Divergence
ETH/BTC at 0.027 (lowest since early 2023). Q1 users +53.5%
QoQ. Glamsterdam (largest upgrade since Merge) in final devnet.
BitMine holds 5.62M ETH (4.66% of supply). 500K ETH left
exchanges last week. MVRV below 0.8 (accumulation zone).
None of this is priced in yet.
4. Crypto is Macro-Correlated
Thursday PCE drives near-term direction. Hot print (core PCE
>3.4%) = dollar strength = $62K BTC re-test. Soft print = potential
relief rally for risk assets including crypto.
