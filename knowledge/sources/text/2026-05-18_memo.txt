Key Themes This Week
Iran / Oil / Geopolitics: Strait of Hormuz remains near-closed. WTI above $107, up 9%+ week-over-week. Drone strikes targeted UAE energy
infrastructure over the weekend. Trump posted Sunday night that Tehran's 'clock is ticking.' Iranian media says talks are deadlocked. U.S. + Israeli
military action possible as early as this week. Oil is the primary inflation driver and the swing variable for all risk assets right now.
Inflation + Fed Transition: CPI 3.8% YoY (highest since Sept 2023). PPI 6% YoY. Rate cut probability: 0%. Rate hike probability this year: ~45%
(was 1% a month ago). 10Y yield at 4.55%, near a one-year high. Kevin Warsh confirmed 54-45 (May 13) -- most partisan Fed chair vote in history.
Powell stays on the board as governor. Warsh's first FOMC as chair: June 16-17. This week: Powell's final meeting minutes drop Wednesday.
Gold Pullback: Gold fell ~4% last week to ~$4,550. Counterintuitive given the geopolitical backdrop, but explained by surging yields and a stronger
dollar raising opportunity cost. WGC Q1 data showed record global demand -- bar/coin buying up 42% YoY, led by Asian investors. Long-term
structural demand is intact; the short-term ceiling holds as long as real yields stay elevated.
